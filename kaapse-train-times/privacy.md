# Privacy Policy — Kaapse Train Times

*Last updated: April 18, 2026*

Kaapse Train Times is an offline timetable for Cape Town Metrorail. Your privacy is simple: we don't collect anything.

## Data collection

Kaapse Train Times does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device with no server component, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device so it works the way you left it:

- Favourite stations
- Recently viewed stations
- Weekday/Sunday timetable preference

This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — Declared by the Android runtime but not used. The app makes no network requests.

## Children's privacy

Kaapse Train Times does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
